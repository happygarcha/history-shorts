## Fact-check list
1. Excavation at Harappa began in 1921 under Daya Ram Sahni; Mohenjo-daro in 1922 (R. D. Banerji); John Marshall announced the new civilisation in 1924.
2. Mature Harappan phase about 2600 to 1900 BCE ("about 4,500 years ago" in the voice is a round figure).
3. The Indus script is undeciphered; average inscription about 4 to 5 signs; no bilingual text. Sign-count estimates differ (about 386 to 694), so the video says only "hundreds of kinds".
